# Sage & Steel: Pinterest pins

Nine ready-to-post pins for a Sage & Steel Pinterest business account. For each one: upload the image, paste the title and description, add the alt text, and paste the link as the pin's destination.

To make more pins later, open `pin-template.html?p=bag` (or any key in it) at 1000 × 1500 and screenshot it, or ask Claude to add one.

## Boards to create first

| Board name | Board description |
|---|---|
| Strength Training for Women | Simple lifting plans, form tips and myth-busting for women who want to get strong. |
