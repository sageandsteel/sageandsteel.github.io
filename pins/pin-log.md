## Rules for new pins

- 1000 × 1500 PNG made from `pin-template.html` in the Sage & Steel style. Check every render for anything cut off before posting.
- No prices (Amazon's rules). No health claims beyond what the site's notes say. No weight-loss promises, no diet-culture language, no body shaming.
- Every description ends with "I earn from qualifying purchases."
- Don't repeat a topic that's already in the posted table unless the angle is clearly new.
